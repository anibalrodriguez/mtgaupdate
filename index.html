About this schedule

Manually curated from official Wizards announcements. Time-limited Arena events only, not permanent queues. Listings include the Reality Fracture schedule, November qualifiers, and the Premier Draft extension through December 1, 2026; this is not an exhaustive or automatically updated feed.

Missing times, prices, and match structures are left unconfirmed. Conflicting dates are separated from the main schedule. Filter counts include current, upcoming, and needs-confirmation listings. Always check the official event page and the Arena client before entering.
