About this schedule

Manually curated from official Wizards announcements. Time-limited Arena events only, not permanent queues. This first version covers the published Reality Fracture schedule through November 10, 2026; it is not an exhaustive or automatically updated feed.

Missing times, prices, and match structures are left unconfirmed. Conflicting dates are separated from the main schedule. Always check the official event page and the Arena client before entering.
